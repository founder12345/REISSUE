# Catalogue acquisition and licensing

**Verified catalogue currently available for launch: 0.** The internal development catalogue has six labelled demonstrations. Synthetic load and workflow fixtures stay in isolated test databases and make no contribution to the verified number. There is no authorised external bulk feed configured.

## Acquisition paths

| Path | Handling |
| --- | --- |
| Brand-supplied historical files | Verify the representative, retain written reuse scope, then review CSV or JSON. Product references and asset permissions remain separate. |
| Licensed dataset or API | Capture the provider agreement, commercial redistribution terms, attribution, data rights, retrieval date and rate limits. Register the source once reviewed. For now, import authorised exports as files. |
| Historical catalogues and manual research | Use primary manufacturer announcements, licensed museum references or brand archives. Write original summaries and record the primary evidence. Confirm the terms for any copied media or archival text. |
| Community contribution | Require a rights attestation and administrator review. Require corroboration before publishing a verification decision. |

Prioritise fashion, outdoor equipment, watches and design. The 1,000, 10,000 and 100,000 catalogue sizes are acquisition targets. Measure progress by counting live, non-demo products with accepted verification and coverage by category. Keep licence evidence and source registration decisions available for audit. A URL passing syntax validation does not establish ownership or permission. The importer stores links and does not fetch arbitrary sources. No integrations are enabled for restricted sources.

The editorial demo photos retain their specific Wikimedia Commons licence and attribution. That reviewed manifest applies to those files only. [Media manifest](docs/archive-media.json)
