# Legal and privacy launch checklist

The French and English privacy copy, Terms, cookie notice, legal notice, community rules, acceptable use, IP and reporting copy are review drafts. A qualified reviewer should match each document to actual operations before the operator publishes it. The production registration gate is an engineering control.

- Confirm the operator’s legal identity, publication and hosting disclosures, contact details, and any applicable registration details. Add them to policy copy and environment configuration. Review the identity and contact required for visitors who cannot sign in.
- Confirm purposes and lawful bases with counsel. Verify who acts as controller or processor for Vercel, PostgreSQL, S3-compatible storage, Resend and Google. Document regions, DPAs, international transfer mechanisms, and notices for each provider.
- Approve the actual data retention schedule. Expired credentials and pending unverified accounts can be removed with the maintenance script. Search events have a 30-day script cutoff. Define retention for private rights cases, legal claims, public content after deletion, audit trails, backups and email service records. Review whether particular content must be retained before acting on a request.
- Test identity verification and escalation for rights requests, including access, rectification, portability, erasure, restriction, objection and withdrawn consent. The account export and deletion controls are immediate. Case review is recorded and requires an operator to execute any restriction or other action outside the app. Establish monitoring, response deadlines and procedure for unsigned-in former users.
- Confirm the intended user age range and countries. Review capacity to accept terms, French consent thresholds, whether parental authorization is appropriate, and how to handle it without collecting unnecessary dates of birth or identity documents.
- Assess DSA applicability to hosting, user posts, catalogue submissions and brand campaigns. Review notice requirements, decisions with reasons, appeal periods, points of contact, recordkeeping and any applicable reporting obligations. The app stores reports and appeals and supports staff review. Define protocol for preserving evidence, hiding confirmed infringing content and notifying unauthenticated reporters through the approved contact channel.
- Audit every photo for rights and provenance. Keep source and licence links with the record. A campaign or catalogue submission includes a rights attestation. Use the administrator upload and moderation workflow for authorised imagery.
- Review GDPR and CNIL cookie requirements against actual deployment. Necessary cookies support sessions and transient OAuth, plus the saved consent choice. Optional analytics is off by default and has equally prominent accept and reject choices when enabled. No marketing SDKs or trackers are installed. Retest after adding any third party.
- Approve versioned Terms with a specific effective date and retention plan. Existing internal accounts were not retroactively marked as having accepted new terms. Define any required re-consent workflow before migrating a public user base.
